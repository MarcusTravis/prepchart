Get-Home Bag

A get-home bag is small. It exists to get you from where you are, work, school or the car, back to your own door when normal transport stops.

Guidance summarized: Nov 19, 2026 · What changed

What it's for

  • A day or less of walking. It's lighter than a 72-hour bag, kept at work or in your car.
  • Ready.gov suggests a work kit with food, water, necessary medicines and comfortable walking shoes, and being ready to shelter at work for at least 24 hours if needed.

What goes in

  • Water and a way to treat more; a few high-calorie snacks.
  • Comfortable walking shoes and socks, a rain layer and an extra warm layer for the season.
  • Headlamp, phone battery pack and cable, a paper map of your route, small first aid kit and any medications.
  • Whistle, a little cash and a card with family contact numbers.

Practice

  • Walk part of your route once on a weekend and time it. Note the places you could stop or find water.
  • Agree on a family plan so people know where you'd go and how you'd check in.
