Hurricane

Hurricanes give days of warning, which is the whole advantage. Use it to decide early whether you're staying or leaving.

Guidance summarized: Nov 19, 2026 · What changed

Before

  • Find out whether you live in an evacuation zone and learn your routes. Local officials, not social media, give evacuation orders.
  • Gather documents, medications, cash, chargers and pet supplies in one place. Fill the gas tank early.
  • Secure outdoor items and know where your nearest shelter is, including whether it accepts pets.

During

  • If you're ordered to evacuate, go. Storm surge from the ocean is a major cause of hurricane deaths and is the reason zone orders exist.
  • If staying, shelter in a small interior room away from windows. Don't go outside during the calm of the eye.

After

  • Stay out of floodwater; it can hide debris and live lines, and moving water can sweep a car away.
  • Run generators outdoors only. Boil or treat water if officials say to.
  • Photograph damage before cleanup and keep receipts.
