Winter Storm

The hazards are cold, slick roads and unsafe heating. Most winter-storm trouble is avoidable by staying put.

Guidance summarized: Nov 19, 2026 · What changed

Heating safetyNever heat a home with a gas oven, grill or camp stove, and never run a generator indoors. Carbon monoxide builds quickly in closed spaces. Keep space heaters well clear of anything that can burn, and don't leave them running unattended.

Before

  • Stock water, food that needs no cooking, medications and warm bedding. Charge devices.
  • Know where your main water shutoff is, and how you'll keep pipes from freezing.
  • Keep your vehicle's tank reasonably full and a winter kit inside.

During

  • Stay home and off the roads. Dress in dry layers; wet clothing loses its warmth.
  • Learn the signs of hypothermia (shivering, confusion, drowsiness) and frostbite (numb, pale skin); both need medical help.
  • Let faucets drip and open cabinet doors on cold nights, as utilities advise.

If stranded in a vehicle

  • Stay with the car; it's easier to find than you are. Run the engine only briefly for warmth, and keep the tailpipe clear of snow to avoid CO buildup. Crack a window.
