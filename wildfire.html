Wildfire

Fires move faster than most people expect. Leaving early is the single most important decision.

Guidance summarized: Nov 19, 2026 · What changed

Before

  • Keep a go-bag ready and know two ways out of your area. Sign up for local alerts.
  • Clear dry brush and debris from around your home as local fire agencies advise.
  • Plan for pets and livestock, and agree on where the family will meet.

During

  • Leave immediately when told to. Don't wait to see the flames.
  • For smoke, an N95-type respirator that fits well helps; cloth and surgical masks do little against fine smoke particles (CDC). Stay indoors with windows closed when air quality is poor, and use a clean-air room or portable HEPA purifier if you have one.
  • Check air quality on AirNow's Fire and Smoke Map.

After

  • Return only when officials say it's safe. Watch for hot spots, damaged trees and slick ash.
  • Wear gloves and a respirator for cleanup; avoid stirring ash dry.
