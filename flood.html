Flood

Floods kill more people in vehicles than almost anywhere else. Moving water is stronger than it looks.

Guidance summarized: Nov 19, 2026 · What changed

Before

  • Check your flood risk and consider flood insurance. Standard homeowner policies usually don't cover flooding, and flood policies generally have a waiting period before they take effect (see FloodSmart).
  • Know the nearest higher ground and your route to it. Keep documents in waterproof containers.

During

  • Move to higher ground early. The National Weather Service slogan is Turn Around, Don't Drown: about 6 inches of fast-moving water can knock an adult down, roughly 12 inches can carry away a small car, and about 2 feet can sweep away an SUV or truck.
  • Don't walk or drive through floodwater.

After

  • Treat floodwater as contaminated: sewage, chemicals and debris. Wash well after contact.
  • Throw away food and water that touched floodwater; follow boil advisories. Don't use electronics or appliances that got wet until checked.
  • Dry out quickly to limit mold, and wear gloves and a respirator for cleanup.
