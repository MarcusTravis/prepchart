Vehicle Kit

Your car is the one kit you always have with you. Ready.gov recommends keeping an emergency supply kit in your vehicle in case you're stranded.

Guidance summarized: Nov 19, 2026 · What changed

Stranded safetyIf you run the engine for heat, keep the tailpipe clear of snow and mud and crack a window; blocked exhaust lets carbon monoxide build up inside the car. Stay with the vehicle in a storm unless safety is only a short, clear walk away.

Always

  • Water and non-perishable snacks, rotated once or twice a year.
  • First aid kit, flashlight or headlamp with spare batteries, and a phone charging cable or battery pack.
  • Jumper cables or a jump pack, a tire inflator or a spare and the tools to change it.
  • Reflective triangles or flares, gloves, a multi-tool, and a paper map.

Winter

  • Warm blanket, extra hat and gloves, ice scraper and a small shovel, and something for traction. Keep the tank at least half full.

Summer

  • Extra water, a sunshade, and a plan for kids and pets; never leave them in a parked car.
