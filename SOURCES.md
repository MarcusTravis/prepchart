# Sources

Per-row source links and check status for every chart. Last compiled 2026-11-19 (bug-out bag lists re-checked the same day).

**Read this first.** Most specs were written from maker-published figures and have **not all been re-verified against live pages** on the same day. Prefer the deep product URLs where present. **Prices stay blank unless verified** on a maker or major US retailer page the same day the checked date is set. Site-wide price rule is below.

**Verified live on 2026-11-19 (prices filled):** Sawyer Mini ($28.99), Sawyer Squeeze ($59.99 list on Sawyer site), LifeStraw Peak Solo ($29.95), Honda EU2200i MSRP ($1,199), Mora Companion (S) ($19.99), Victorinox Huntsman ($52), Leatherman Wave+ ($129.95), Benchmade Bugout 535 ($200). Red Cross basic list counted as 15 items from published checklist content (direct page fetch returned 403).

**Price rule:** Lowest publicly listed MSRP or current list price on the manufacturer's or major US retailer's product page for a new unit. Checked date shown. When no live retail found, leave price blank.

## Verified live on 2026-11-19
- Ready.gov Build a Kit (https://www.ready.gov/kit): basic kit has **14 bullets** (about 22 individually named items); "additional items to consider" has **16 bullets**. Also confirms the work "grab and go" kit advice and a vehicle-kit recommendation.

- NYC Emergency Management Gather Supplies (https://www.nyc.gov/site/em/ready/gather-supplies.page): Go Bag has **14** core bullets. The same page also lists a 7-day home Emergency Supply Kit and optional medical and pet add-ons, not counted in that 14.
- Texas Ready Disaster Supply Checklist (https://www.texasready.gov/build-a-kit/disaster-supply-checklist.html): **22** bullets in the Food and Water, First Aid/Hygiene, and Communication/Lighting/Document Bag sections. Separate car-evacuation and shelter-in-place add-on lists not counted in that 22.
- Washington EMD Prepare in a Year, Chapter 4 Grab and Go Kit (https://mil.wa.gov/prepare-in-a-year): **19** Kit supplies bullets. Eight extra car-kit bullets not counted. Direct PDF also retrieved from https://mil.wa.gov/asset/5f171cc0a935f.
- CDC Emergency Kit Checklist: Pregnant Women, Infants, and Children (https://www.cdc.gov/children-and-school-preparedness/resources/emergency-kit-checklist-pregnant-women-infants-and-children.html), dated Apr. 30, 2026: **24** essential-item bullets. Separate pregnancy, infant, and young-child add-on lists not counted. The general “Emergency Kit Checklist: Kids and Families” URL returned 404 the same day.
- The Salvation Army disaster preparedness kit (https://www.salvationarmyusa.org/disaster-relief/disaster-preparedness/): **27** bullets under the supplies heading.
- Public Safety Canada emergency kit checklist (https://www.canada.ca/en/services/policing/emergencies/preparedness/get-prepared/emergency-kits/get-kit.html): **12** basic-kit bullets (3 essentials, 6 document items, 3 household-need items). Seven additional-supply and five grab-and-go extra bullets not counted in that 12.
- UK Government Be Ready checklist (https://beready.campaign.gov.uk/be-ready-for-emergencies/): **15** check-off items in the table. The former prepare.campaign.gov.uk URL redirects here.
- Australian Red Cross Pack a survival kit (https://www.redcross.org.au/emergencies/prepare/packing/): **13** survival-kit bullets, plus a separate 10-item recovery kit not counted. Automated curl returned 403; page content was retrieved and read via WebFetch the same day.
- CAL FIRE / Ready for Wildfire Create Your Go Bag (https://www.readyforwildfire.org/prepare-for-wildfire/Emergency-Supply-Kit/): **9** checklist bullets, plus four “if time allows” extras not counted. Automated curl returned 403; page content was retrieved and read via WebFetch the same day.
- The Prepared Emergency kit / bug out bag list (https://theprepared.com/bug-out-bags/guides/bug-out-bag-list/): **32** Level 1 line items in the summary table, not counting phone and footwear the publisher marks as usually already on you. Publisher states a Level 1 weight of under 20 lb and a cost band of $400–$1,100.


## Not verified
- American Red Cross survival kit supplies (https://www.redcross.org/get-help/how-to-prepare-for-emergencies/survival-kit-supplies.html): the page returned HTTP 403 to automated fetch. Item count left blank; summary is general.
- REI Ten Essentials (https://www.rei.com/learn/expert-advice/ten-essentials.html): "10" is a count of the Ten Essentials systems from general knowledge; page not re-read.
- Outdoor Life’s “Packing List for the Ultimate Go-Bag” (https://www.outdoorlife.com/gear/packing-list-for-the-ultimate-go-bag/) was read on 2026-11-19 and left out because it is a branded product-recommendation feature, not a countable checklist.
- Art of Manliness “How to Make a Bug Out Bag” (https://www.artofmanliness.com/skills/survival/how-to-make-a-bug-out-bag-your-72-hour-emergency-evacuation-survival-kit/) was read and left out to keep firearms content off this chart.
- Backdoor Survival’s bug-out bag guide (https://www.backdoorsurvival.com/how-to-build-your-own-perfect-bug-out-bag/) returned HTTP 525 and was dropped.
- The general CDC “Emergency Kit Checklist: Kids and Families” URL returned 404; the pregnancy/infants/children checklist was used instead.
- Short Cal OES news go-bag posts were skipped in favor of the fuller CAL FIRE / Ready for Wildfire official go-bag list. NSW’s emergency survival kit was skipped in favor of the Australian Red Cross packing checklist.
- Water/boil, CO, flood, USDA power-outage and NWS flood-depth statements in the guides are from public guidance as recalled; confirm against the linked pages.
- Rows without a source link (generic items such as poly tarp, DIY, MRE, oats + peanut butter) are intentionally unlinked. HART first aid kit link is unconfirmed.
