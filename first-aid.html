First aid disclaimerThis chart is general information, not medical advice. A kit does not replace training or professional care. For serious injury or illness, call 911 (or your local emergency number). Take a first aid, CPR or Stop the Bleed course, check medications and supplies for expiry, and account for allergies (including latex) before you rely on a kit.
