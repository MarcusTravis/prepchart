More bug-out bag lists

November 19, 2026

The bug-out bag comparison now covers 14 published lists, up from four. Added NYC Emergency Management, Texas Ready, Washington EMD, the CDC (pregnancy, infants, and children), the Salvation Army, Canada’s GetPrepared, the UK Be Ready checklist, the Australian Red Cross, CAL FIRE / Ready for Wildfire, and The Prepared’s Level 1 packing list. Item counts are taken from each page; weight and cost stay blank unless the publisher states a figure.
